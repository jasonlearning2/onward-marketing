The Reality

School leaders are not short on initiatives.
They are short on support for sustaining them.

The Kickoff Trap

Vision statements. Stakeholder meetings. Excitement. Then momentum dies within weeks. Over 60% of change management initiatives fail — not because the plan was wrong, but because the daily work of sustaining change had no structure.

Not a Tech Problem

AI is transforming schools faster than leaders can manage. But this is not a technology problem — it is a human behavior and change management challenge that requires a fundamentally different kind of support.

Leading Alone

Between the kickoff meeting and the results, leaders navigate resistance, build coalitions, shift culture, and sustain momentum — usually with no daily support and no structured companion for the journey.

The 12-Week Journey

The Onward Arc

A structured change management journey built on ADKAR, Kotter, and the Knoster Model — translated into daily school leadership language.

01

Clarity

Weeks 1–2

Why this change? Who is affected? What does success look like?

02

Coalition

Weeks 3–4

Build buy-in. Identify resisters, allies, and champions.

03

Momentum

Weeks 5–8

Daily microactions. Remove obstacles. Celebrate small wins.

04

Roots

Weeks 9–12

Embed the change. Prevent backslide. Institutionalize the new.
